    About me

    My research spans high-power fibre lasers and amplifiers, nonlinear and ultrafast optics, and fibre-based sensors and devices. I also teach mathematics.

    I work in the Photonics research group at The Hague University of Applied Sciences. I am employed by Navitas and teach mathematics at Twente Pathway College in Enschede. I earned my PhD at the University of Southampton’s Optoelectronics Research Centre and worked in industry at Southampton Photonics and Xtera Communications. Short CV

    Research interests

    High-power fibre lasers and amplifiers

    My doctoral and published work examined cladding-pumped ytterbium and erbium–ytterbium fibre lasers, including high-energy Q-switched operation.

    High-energy Q-switched ytterbium fibre laser · Optics Letters, 2000

    Nonlinear and ultrafast optics

    My co-authored research has examined transitions between mode-locked and noise-like pulse regimes in multimode fibre lasers, and nonlinear refraction in transparent conducting oxide films near epsilon-near-zero conditions.

    Mode locking and noise-like pulses · Optics Letters, 2026
    Nonlinear refraction of epsilon-near-zero films · Optics Express, 2023

    Optical sensing and fibre devices

    At The Hague University of Applied Sciences, I work on a methane absorption demonstrator and lead the university’s contribution to characterising fibre Bragg grating sensors for composite strain. My published work also includes fibre laser sensing and plasma-based fibre taper fabrication.

    Review of fibre laser sensors · Photonics, 2023
    Plasma-based optical fibre tapering rig · HardwareX, 2024
